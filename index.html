02 — ABOUT ME

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I turn ideas into clean, interactive digital experiences.

I'm John Leonard, a Front-End Developer and UI Designer who enjoys the space where design decisions meet working code. I care about interfaces that are fast, accessible, and easy to navigate — built with clean markup, deliberate typography, and just enough motion to feel alive.

Most of my work sits between design and development: translating a layout idea into a responsive, production-ready interface, then refining it until every state — hover, focus, empty, loading — feels intentional.
